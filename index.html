About Us

At Leeward Exports Inc., we specialize in the shipments of personal, household, and commercial goods from Canada to the Leeward Islands. Built on reliability, trust, and customer satisfaction, we provide dependable ocean cargo shipping with clear schedules and estimated arrival dates. Previously operated under Total Sales Inc., our company has evolved to better reflect the islands we proudly serve and the communities we support. With over 25 years of experience, we continue to be a trusted cargo shipper and exporter committed to professional service and customer care.

Vision

To provide the most reliable ocean cargo shipping and export services to the Leeward Islands. To build trusted relationships with our clients that demonstrate our appreciation of their business. To work with a local broker at each shipping port to ensure our customers have a wholesome and efficient shipping experience.
